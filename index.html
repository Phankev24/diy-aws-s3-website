About me

I'm a student at Kristiania University College getting hands-on with DevOps. This site is part of a lab exercise on hosting static content in the cloud.

Why S3?

Static files need storage, not servers. S3 is cheap, durable, and can serve a website directly once public access and a bucket policy are set up.

What I'm learning

  • Bucket policies public read access
